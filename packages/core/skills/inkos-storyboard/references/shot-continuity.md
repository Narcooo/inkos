# Shot continuity

## Shots serve the beat

Use a new shot when information, power, attention, spatial understanding, or emotion changes. Do not convert every sentence into a shot and do not collapse a full dramatic turn into one decorative image.

## Visual continuity

Track screen direction, character position, eyelines, props, lighting, time, and location identity across adjacent shots. Repeated locations should preserve stable visual anchors unless the story explicitly changes them.

## Image prompts remain executable

Describe the visible moment, subjects, composition, camera, environment, light, and required continuity anchors. Avoid abstract story summaries. The prompt should depict the selected beat without adding text, characters, or objects not present in the storyboard.
